# Car Market & Pricing Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

**Car Market & Pricing Analysis** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze car listings and identify patterns in vehicle pricing, brands, fuel types, transmission types, ownership, vehicle age, and mileage.

The dashboard transforms raw car listing data into meaningful visual insights that can help understand pricing trends and characteristics of the used-car market.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the overall car market and listing volume
* Understand average and median asking prices
* Compare brands based on average asking price
* Analyze car listings by transmission type
* Identify yearly trends in car listings
* Compare asking prices across different fuel types
* Understand the distribution of cars by vehicle age
* Analyze cars based on owner type
* Examine average kilometers driven

## 📌 Key KPIs

* **Total Cars:** 9.582K

* **Average Car Age:** 7.64 years

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Top 10 Brands by Average Asking Price

Compares the average asking price of the top 10 car brands and highlights the brands positioned at the higher end of the market.

### 2. Cars by Transmission Type

Shows the distribution of cars between **Manual** and **Automatic** transmission types.

### 3. Car Listings by Year

Analyzes how the number of car listings varies across different manufacturing years.

### 4. Average Asking Price by Fuel Type

Compares the average asking price of **Diesel, Petrol, and Hybrid/CNG** vehicles.

### 5. Car Listings by Age

Shows the distribution of vehicles across different age groups.

### 6. Cars by Owner Type

Compares listings based on ownership type such as first-owner and second-owner vehicles.
